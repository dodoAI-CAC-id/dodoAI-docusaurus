# Guide
- Please ensure this document covers all items listed below.
- The following sections serve as a guide. Please use your own wording and do not copy them verbatim.

When defining a business requirement, be sure to clearly state the objectives, business context, stakeholders, requirements (what needs to be achieved), functional requirements, business flows and rules, quality expectations, constraints, success criteria, and exclusions. Focus on "what" the business needs, not "how" to implement solutions. Use language that is understandable for all business-side stakeholders.

Recommended sections:

## 1. Background & Objectives
Describe why this requirement is necessary and what business goals are being addressed.

## 2. Stakeholders
List all relevant parties involved, such as business owners, users, and related departments.

## 3. Business Requirements (What)
Clearly define what needs to be achieved from a business perspective. Avoid describing implementation methods.

## 4. Functional Requirements
Specify the concrete functions and features the system or solution must provide, linked to the business requirements.

## 5. Business Processes & Rules
Outline the key business processes, workflows, and any specific business rules that must be followed.

## 6. Quality Requirements & Constraints
Specify quality expectations (such as performance, reliability, security) and list any constraints (such as deadlines, budgets, or regulatory compliance).

## 7. Success Criteria & KPIs
Define how to measure the success of this requirement, including KPIs or other concrete indicators.

## 8. Exclusions (Out of Scope)
State any items or requirements explicitly out of scope for this document.
